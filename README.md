# 🏥 Patient Churn Analysis in Healthcare

## 📌 Project Overview

Patient retention is a critical challenge in the healthcare industry. Losing patients not only impacts revenue but also reflects gaps in service quality, accessibility, and engagement.

This project analyzes patient behavior and service patterns to identify the **key drivers of patient churn** and provide **actionable business recommendations** to improve retention.

---

## 🎯 Objective

* Identify factors contributing to patient churn
* Analyze the impact of engagement, cost, experience, and accessibility
* Provide data-driven recommendations to reduce churn and improve patient retention

---

## 📊 Churn Definition

Churn is defined as the **loss of patient engagement**, where a patient does not return for services within a specified time window.

* Churn is modeled as a **binary variable (1 = churned, 0 = active)**
* A **time-based approach** is used, based on the recency of the patient’s last visit
* Patients with no visits beyond a defined threshold are classified as churned

This approach reflects real-world healthcare behavior, where prolonged inactivity indicates disengagement.

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 1. 📱 Low Engagement Drives Churn

* Patients with fewer visits and more missed appointments are more likely to churn
* Low portal usage is linked to disengagement

---

### 2. 💳 High Costs Increase Churn Risk

* Higher out-of-pocket expenses strongly correlate with patient churn
* Financial burden reduces long-term retention

---

### 3. ⏰ Patient Experience Matters

* Long wait times negatively impact satisfaction
* Staff interaction has a stronger influence on retention than provider ratings

---

### 4. 📍 Accessibility Impacts Retention

* Patients living farther from healthcare facilities are more likely to churn
* Convenience plays a key role in continued engagement

---

## 🚀 Business Recommendations

### 📱 Improve Engagement

* Implement appointment reminders (SMS/email)
* Promote patient portal usage
* Schedule follow-ups proactively

---

### 💳 Reduce Financial Barriers

* Offer flexible payment plans
* Improve cost transparency
* Support high-cost patients with targeted programs

---

### ⏰ Enhance Patient Experience

* Optimize scheduling to reduce wait times
* Improve staff training and service quality
* Act on patient feedback

---

### 📍 Increase Accessibility

* Expand telehealth services
* Enable remote consultations
* Improve access for distant patients

---

### 🔔 Target At-Risk Patients

* Identify high-risk patients based on behavior patterns
* Launch re-engagement campaigns (reminders, offers, checkups)

---

## 🧠 Final Insight

Patient churn is driven by a combination of:

* **Behavioral factors** (engagement)
* **Financial barriers** (cost)
* **Service quality** (experience)
* **Logistical challenges** (distance)

A proactive, data-driven approach can significantly improve **patient retention, satisfaction, and long-term value**.
